Before you write

A few useful answers.

If your question is already here, it might save us both an email. If not, the form is the right place.

What should I include in my note?

Context, dates, audience, and what you're hoping to get from the conversation. If it's a speaking request, a link to the event or a short brief helps a lot.

Can I invite you to speak?

Yes. I speak on education access, youth leadership, and the post-2030 education agenda. Include the date, location, audience, and format, and I'll come back to you with availability.

Do you take mentoring conversations?

Yes, when there's room. Students and early-career organisers are welcome to write. I reply when I can, and I'm honest when I can't.

How quickly do you reply?

Usually within 3–5 working days. If something is time-sensitive, say so in the first line and I'll do my best to prioritise it.
